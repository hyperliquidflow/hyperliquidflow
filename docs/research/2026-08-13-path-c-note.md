## The asymmetry that emerged today

Two numbers describe the positioning factor, and they have drifted a long way
apart:

- **The diagnostic**: IC 0.0641 at t 3.13, frozen against survivorship, orthogonal
  to momentum at one, three and seven days, to volume, to volume change and to
  funding. Four dumb rivals offered the job across two studies, none able to do
  it.
- **The traded book**: +39.0 bps a day at t 1.42, capacity around $100k before
  the thin names exhaust, worth roughly $364 a day if it is real.

The ranking is strong and the trade is weak. That gap is not a disappointment,
it is the shape of the opportunity, and it points somewhere different from where
this project has been pointing.

## Why the gap exists

Converting a ranking into a book costs three things that the ranking itself does
not pay: the spread and fees on every rebalance, the funding on every held leg,
and the depth limit of the coins the ranking likes. The measurement says roughly
a fifth of the gross edge goes to execution between a $10k and a $100k book, and
past that the strategy changes character rather than scaling.

**A ranking has no capacity ceiling. A book does.** Selling the same information
to a hundred people costs nothing extra and is not degraded by their trading,
whereas trading it yourself is capped near $100k by the order books of STBL and
RENDER.

## What this changes about the paths

**Path A (trade it) is capped at good personal income.** Roughly $364 a day at
the ceiling, if the edge is real, which is about $11k a month. That is worth
having and it is not a business. It also carries the full burden of proof: the
traded book needs until roughly day 283 of forward data to clear a powered bar,
because a t 1.42 effect is expensive to confirm.

**Path C (sell the intelligence) is now better supported by the evidence than it
was yesterday**, for three reasons that all emerged today:

1. The asset it would sell is the diagnostic, and the diagnostic is the strong
   number. Path C does not need the traded book to clear t 2.5; it needs the
   ranking to be real and useful, which is a lower and already better-supported
   bar.
2. Its capacity is not bounded by alt-perp depth.
3. The forward shadow record started on 2026-08-12 **is** the published track
   record Path C requires. The launch precondition of 90 days of live
   performance is already accumulating at zero cost, whichever path is chosen.

The honest counterweight: Path C is a different job. It is marketing, support and
retention rather than research, the unit economics in the April model only work
at midpoint assumptions, and it needs roughly $150k of working capital to ramp
seriously. Nothing about today makes that easier. What changed is only which
asset is worth selling.

## The recommendation, unchanged in sequence and changed in emphasis

Keep running Path A's clock, because it costs nothing and the forward record
serves both paths identically. But stop treating the traded book as the only
definition of success. **If the day-60 diagnostic checkpoint clears its powered
bar while the traded book stays marginal, that is not a failure, it is Path C's
green light arriving before Path A's.**

Worth deciding in advance, while it is still hypothetical: if the ranking proves
real and the trade stays capacity-bound, is the goal personal income or a
business? The answer changes what gets built next, and it is much easier to
answer now than after the day-60 number exists and is pulling at the decision.
